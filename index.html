VSOP87 Ephemeris (NASA JPL)

Base planetary positions are calculated using the VSOP87 (Variations Séculaires des Orbites Planétaires) planetary theory. This is the exact same mathematical model developed by the Bureau des Longitudes in Paris and utilized by NASA for calculating high-precision orbital vectors in our solar system.

True Lahiri Ayanamsa

Vedic astrology requires Sidereal (Nirayana) coordinates, while modern astronomy outputs Tropical (Sayana) coordinates. The engine bridges this gap by calculating the exact Chitra Paksha (Lahiri) Ayanamsa for the given Julian Date to account for the precession of the equinoxes (~50.2 seconds of arc per year).

ayanamsa = 23.856944 + (1.395833 * T) + (0.000083 * T²);

Spherical Trigonometry (Lagna)

The Ascendant (Lagna) is the exact degree of the zodiac rising on the eastern horizon at the specific latitude and longitude of birth. The engine dynamically calculates the Local Sidereal Time (LST) and the Obliquity of the Ecliptic, processing them through spherical trigonometric functions (atan2, sine, cosine) to pinpoint the Lagna down to the arcminute.

4

Parashari Varga Mechanics

Divisional charts (Vargas) are not merely mathematical harmonics. The engine applies the strict, classical rules outlined by Sage Parashara. For example, in the Navamsa (D9), a sign is divided into 9 segments of 3°20'. A planet in the 1st segment of a Fiery sign maps to Aries; in an Earthy sign, it maps to Capricorn. All 19 Vargas utilize these exact mapping arrays.
